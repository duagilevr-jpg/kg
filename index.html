Commercial & lifestyle center

Everyday Convenience. A New Destination.

At the heart of the King’s Garden Collection vision is a major commercial and lifestyle center, planned to bring together retail, dining, services and spaces for social interaction.

More than a place to shop, it is intended to become an active part of neighborhood life: a place to meet friends, spend time with family, enjoy a meal or access the services that make everyday living easier.
